Job search · runs entirely in your browser

The latest jobs, matched to you

Type a role, or paste your resume and let it pull out your skills. It queries open job-board APIs at the same time, merges and de-duplicates the results, scores every posting against your skills and preferences, and puts the strongest matches first. Plus one-click search links for Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed and the rest.

Why aren’t Naukri, LinkedIn and Indeed results listed inline? Those sites forbid automated scraping and have no open feed. This page pulls live postings from boards that publish an open API (Remotive, Arbeitnow, RemoteOK, Jobicy, The Muse) and gives you a pre-filled search for everything else. Your resume text stays in your browser; only the short role keyword is sent to a job board. See Data sources.
